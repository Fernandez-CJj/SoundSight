## Notes for future captures

- The conversion screenshot says **Recording converted**. It demonstrates the
  recording conversion flow, not recognition from a photographed printed sheet.
- The three practice screenshots contain Wrong, Missed, or Try again feedback.
  They accurately show the current app. For a later presentation pass, captures
  with correct-note feedback would show a more successful practice moment.
- Keep the app in its light theme and natural orientation. Use original-resolution
  PNG or JPG captures; do not stretch landscape screens into portrait frames.
